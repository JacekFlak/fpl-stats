📊 Detailed Analysis

Our AI uses an advanced algorithm powered by Opta professional statistics to predict player performance over the next 5 gameweeks.

Main Formula

xP = (0.1×Form + 0.25×PPG + 0.4×Opta + 0.25×xGI) × Difficulty × Availability × 5

🔥 Form Component (10%)

Recent performance trend from last gameweeks

Form Score from FPL API

📈 Points Per Game (25%)

Season average performance baseline

Total Points ÷ Games Played

⚽ Opta Component (40%)

Professional statistics from official Premier League data provider:

  • Expected Goals (xG) × 5 - Shot quality analysis
  • Expected Assists (xA) × 3 - Chance creation probability
  • Influence ÷ 100 - Match impact metric
  • Creativity ÷ 100 - Offensive contribution
  • Threat ÷ 100 - Attacking danger posed
  • ICT Index ÷ 50 - Combined statistical index

🎯 xGI Component (25%)

Direct attacking contributions (DEF, MID, FWD only)

(xG + xA) per 90 minutes

Adjustment Factors

🏟️ Fixture Difficulty

  • Easy (FDR ≤ 2): 1.4× multiplier
  • Medium (FDR = 3): 1.0× baseline
  • Hard (FDR ≥ 4): 0.65× reduced

⏱️ Playing Time Factor

Based on average minutes per game:

  • 60+ min/game: 1.0× (regular starter)
  • 45-60 min/game: 0.75× (rotation risk)
  • 30-45 min/game: 0.5× (high rotation)
  • <30 min/game: 0.3× (bench player)

Also adjusted by injury status (chance of playing %)

💰 Value Rating

Value = xP ÷ Price (£m)

Identifies best expected points per million spent

🛡️ Clean Sheet Probability (xCS) - For Defenders & Goalkeepers

Defenders and goalkeepers earn most points from clean sheets (4 pts). Our algorithm calculates Expected Clean Sheets (xCS) based on:

Fixture Difficulty Impact

  • Easy (FDR ≤2): 50% base + team CS record
  • Medium (FDR=3): 30% base + team CS record
  • Hard (FDR≥4): 15% base + team CS record

Team Defensive Record

Historical clean sheets per game for the team

Clean Sheets ÷ Games Started
xCS Points = CS Probability × 4 points

Goalkeeper Bonus: Expected save points added (avg saves per game ÷ 3)

🔄 Transfer Recommendation Criteria

Transfers are suggested when the replacement player has:

  • ≥ 10% higher xP than current squad player
  • Price fits within your available budget
  • Player is available (not injured, chance of playing > 0%)
  • Player not already in your squad
